Historical Overview

Pakistan was created in 1947 as a result of the partition of British India. Since independence, it has experienced political transitions, military rule periods, and democratic developments.

  • 1947 – Independence from British India
  • 1971 – Separation of East Pakistan (Bangladesh)
  • 1998 – Nuclear tests conducted
  • 2000s – War on Terror involvement
  • Present – Economic reforms & geopolitical balancing
