Historical Overview

India has one of the world's oldest civilizations and became independent from British rule in 1947. Since independence, it has developed into a major global power in technology, economy, and geopolitics.

  • 1947 – Independence & Partition
  • 1971 – Bangladesh Liberation War
  • 1991 – Economic Liberalization
  • 1998 – Nuclear Tests
  • Present – Rapid economic & technological growth
