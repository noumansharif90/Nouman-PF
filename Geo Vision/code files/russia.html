Historical Overview

Russia has a long imperial history, followed by the Soviet Union era, and modern federation state formation in 1991. It remains one of the most influential geopolitical actors globally.

  • 1917 – Russian Revolution
  • 1922–1991 – Soviet Union Era
  • 1991 – USSR Collapse & Russian Federation formed
  • 2000s – Economic and military rebuilding
  • Present – Global strategic competition era
