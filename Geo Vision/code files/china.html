Historical Overview

China is one of the world’s oldest civilizations with thousands of years of history. In modern times, it transformed rapidly after economic reforms in 1978 into a global industrial and economic powerhouse.

  • 1949 – People's Republic of China established
  • 1978 – Economic reforms & opening-up policy
  • 2001 – WTO membership
  • 2010s – Rise as global manufacturing leader
  • Present – Tech, trade & military expansion
