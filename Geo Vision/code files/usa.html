Historical Overview

The United States became independent in 1776 and rapidly grew into one of the most powerful nations in modern history, especially after World War II, when it emerged as a global superpower.

  • 1776 – Independence declared
  • 1861–1865 – Civil War
  • 1945 – Post WWII superpower emergence
  • Cold War era – Global influence expansion
  • Present – Tech & military dominance
